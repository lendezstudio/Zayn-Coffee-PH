Our Cafe

A small space that got a clean, modern refresh.

Zayn Coffee PH sits in Eastern Barangay, Hilongos, a stop for locals on their way to work and for travelers passing through Leyte. The space was reworked into a whitewashed, modern interior with an outdoor patio, the look most people describe as clean, comfortable, and welcoming.

Behind the counter is a proper espresso setup and a menu that mixes familiar coffee-shop staples with a few distinctly local, playful choices. Behind every reply online is the same warm, recognizable voice, usually signed off with two words: brew much.

How Zayn Started

From a small roadside stall to the space it is today.

Zayn Coffee began simple: a covered counter under a thatched roof, string lights, and a handful of seats. The space has since been rebuilt into the clean, whitewashed cafe you can visit now, same spirit, more room to sit and stay.

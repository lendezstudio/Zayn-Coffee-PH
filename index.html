Our Story

From Do it Lattè to Zayn Coffee

May 8, 2023

Zayn Coffee began on May 8, 2023, as a simple online coffee business called “Do it Lattè.”

What started as an online coffee venture eventually grew into a bigger dream. Two baristas from the Philippines and Indonesia came together through a shared passion for coffee and decided to create a physical space of their own.

From there, Zayn Coffee was born.

A Space to Slow Down

We envisioned Zayn Coffee as a cozy, rustic tropical coffee shop with a warm and inviting character.

With a traditional thatched roof, earthy wood, soft cream tones, and metal details, our space brings together a relaxed and bohemian atmosphere where people can slow down, connect, and enjoy great coffee.

  • Thatched roof
  • Earthy wood
  • Soft cream tones
  • Metal details

Why Zayn?

Good Coffee, With a Meaning Close to Home

The word “Zayn” comes from Arabic and means “good.” It also carries a special meaning for us because Zayn is our son’s nickname.

For us, Zayn Coffee simply means:

Good Coffee.

Our Mission

Coffee at the Heart of It All

Our mission is to introduce great coffee and an international coffee shop experience to the people of Hilongos and communities across Leyte.

We want Zayn Coffee to be more than a place to grab a cup. We want it to be a space where people can discover and appreciate good coffee, experience thoughtful brewing and coffee culture, and create meaningful moments.

At the heart of everything we do, Zayn Coffee is all about coffee.
